Korea Travel Essentials

Crucial tips on navigation, payments, and dining etiquette for seamless local travel.

🗺️

Navigation Reality

Google Maps has limited walking & driving directions in Korea due to local spatial data regulations. Always install Naver Map or KakaoMap for accurate bus schedules and pedestrian routing.

Must-Have App
💳

Cashless & Transit Cards

Korea is nearly 100% cashless, and overseas Visa/Mastercard cards are widely accepted. However, transit cards like Climate Card or T-money can only be reloaded with physical cash at convenience stores.

Keep ₩30,000 Cash
🛎️

Restaurant Table Buzzers

Instead of raising your hand or waving, check the edge of your dining table for a small call button (벨). Press it once to request service, utensils, or your final check.

Dining Etiquette
⚡

High-Speed KTX Trains

Book regional intercity trains (Gangneung, Gyeongju, Andong) at least 2-3 weeks in advance via the official Korail Talk app, as peak weekend departures sell out early.

Advance Booking
☕

MZ Cafe Culture

Trendy regional cafes often enforce a strict "1 Drink per Person" policy. Single-use plastic cups are banned inside; if you plan to stay indoors, choose "For Here" (매장용) at the order kiosk.

1 Drink per Person
🚨

Emergency & Translation

Dial 1330 for the official Korea Travel Helpline (24/7, multi-language support). For on-the-fly signs and menu OCR translation, download Papago.

Call 1330 for Help
